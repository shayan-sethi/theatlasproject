About Atlas

Atlas is an editorial project that explores politics, political and philosophical theory through a geographical lens, alongside independent arguments. We examine how power, ideas, economics, society, and culture interact across regions. Our aim is to delve a little deeper than mere headlines and explore the systems that run our world, article by article.

Editorial & Leadership

Adi Narayanan Jha

Founder, Editor & Writer

Adi founded The Atlas Project as a student-led publication designed to explore the political, economic and social forces that shape our world. He writes and edits all pieces, which aim to make global affairs and complex political and philosophical theory accessible without stripping them of their complexity.
